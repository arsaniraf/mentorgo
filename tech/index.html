Frequently Asked Questions

What is MentorGo and what makes it different?

MentorGo is Indonesia’s first integrated tourism ecosystem company. We don’t just build businesses — we develop sustainable ecosystems that connect tourism, local culture, environment, and economy through smart, strategic collaboration.

How can MentorGo help grow my tourism business?

We offer tailored support through specialized business units — from infrastructure, marketing, hospitality, transportation, retail, to sustainability — all designed to elevate your destination or brand within a connected ecosystem.

What kinds of organizations do you work with?

We collaborate with local governments, private businesses, NGOs, tourism operators, and investors who are passionate about building impactful, long-term tourism initiatives.

What kind of partnership models do you offer?

From co-creation and managed services to joint ventures and licensing — we offer flexible, scalable models based on your goals, budget, and vision. We grow with you, not just around you.

Is MentorGo suitable for small or early-stage projects?

Absolutely. Whether you're just starting out or scaling up, our modular approach allows us to support projects of all sizes — from remote village tourism to world-class destinations.

How do you ensure impact and sustainability?

Through units like MentorGreen and MentorLand, we integrate environmental conservation, community empowerment, and cultural preservation into every project. Growth should leave a legacy, not just a footprint.

Where does MentorGo operate?

We are based in the Mentawai Islands, but our model is built for scale. We are open to partnerships across Indonesia — especially in regions rich in natural and cultural tourism potential.

How do we get started with MentorGo?

Let’s begin with a conversation. We’ll explore your needs, goals, and challenges, then recommend the best partnership model to move forward — aligned with your vision, powered by our expertise.
