Who Are Offshore Python Developers and How You Can Hire Them

Offshore Python developers are tech experts based in countries other than their hiring companies. They contribute to projects remotely as an extension of an in-house tech team or as a newly-assembled one.

Nearshore vs Offshore Python Software Development Experts: What’s the Difference?

Collaborating with offshore developers assumes contracting professionals from distant regions and implies significant time and cultural differences. For a US company, offshore locations include, for example, India, the Philippines, or Romania.

Nearshoring involves assembling or extending teams in neighboring countries with similar time zones and shared cultural behaviors. So acquiring tech talent nearby is often associated with easier communication. Countries like Canada, Mexico, and Colombia are the nearshore destinations for the US.

Nearshore locations are beneficial for in-person meetings, project management, cultural alignment, and legal considerations due to overlapping business hours, and possible similarities in the legal framework. However, offshore regions can offer more reasonable fees and a more established talent pool than nearby countries.

Tasks that Offshore Python Developer Can Perform for You

Remote coders with deep expertise in Python can add significant value to your projects in the following areas:

  • Building web apps and APIs. Python devs create robust and scalable solutions using Django and Flask frameworks. They build catchy and functional UIs, secure backend operations, and ensure smooth integration with databases and external services.
  • Creating AI/ML models. Developers gather and preprocess data to build and train algorithms with libraries like Keras, TensorFlow, and PyTorch. These experts drive innovation by enabling automation, predictive analytics, and AI-driven decisions. 
  • Data engineering. Experts in the Python programming language will help you design, create, and manage data pipelines with Pandas, SQLAlchemy, and Apache Spark libraries. They efficiently extract and manipulate data to process and analyze large datasets.
  • Task automation. The Selenium, Celery, and Airflow libraries help Python specialists create scripts that automate routine tasks and manage workflows. This boosts your team’s productivity, allowing managers to focus on high-level activities while reducing operational costs. 
  • Creating cloud systems. Python developers excel at designing, deploying, and securely managing cloud infrastructures using Azure, AWS, and Google Cloud platforms. They help businesses cut costs, easily access data, enhance collaboration, and more. 
  • Incorporating ERP solutions. Professionals who implement solutions like SAP, Oracle, Tryton ERP, and other systems help businesses enhance productivity and stay competitive. They customize and integrate ERP solutions to align them with your needs. 

Companies can get high-quality and cost-effective solutions by leveraging expertise in Python development in these key areas. Professionals with deep knowledge of Python will enhance productivity, drive innovation, and boost your business’s competitiveness.

3 Ways to Collaborate With Offshore Python Development Teams

There are three main formats for engaging remote Python experts that depend on your budget, management preferences, and project requirements.

  1. Freelance developers. Companies find and contract them through freelancing platforms, such as Upwork, Toptal, Fiverr, etc. This model is widely used for small projects and one-off tasks, and a client handles the project management. However, control over these specialists and their commitment and ability to follow deadlines can become a constraint. 
  2. Local recruiting + hiring through EOR (Employer of Record). Once you plan to employ developers in another country, you can engage local tech recruiters to find and vet the candidates there. Then, an EOR hires them, handling all the employment issues while you get complete control over your offshore development team.  
  3. Dedicated development team (DDT) providers. Staff augmentation partners embrace two functions: your local recruiter and an official employer. However, they go much further by sharing the responsibility for the retention of your developers and continuously addressing your business needs.  

Understanding each model’s peculiarities will help you select the one that best suits your project needs and will drive its success.

NOTE

Some companies also consider outsourcing as a way of hiring developers. However, outsourcing software development means delegating the entire development process to a third party while you pay for the offshore Python development services and get a turnkey solution. Though this model assumes developers work on your assignment, you don’t collaborate with them directly or manage their workload, so we’ve excluded it from the list above.

Cost of Python Developers in Different Countries

Let’s review the average salary ranges of offshore Python programmers in several key locations. (Glassdoor, DOU)

Eastern Europe, Asia, and Latin America offer, on average, 30%-60% lower salaries due to lower living costs in these regions. However, it’s essential to consider time zones and cultural differences when selecting the location of your offshore Python developers.

Factors Influencing the Compensation of Offshore Python Developers

There are a few criteria that impact the cost of hiring offshore Python developers:

  1. Experience: Senior Python developers handle more challenging tasks like architecting complex systems, advanced data analysis, and leading dev teams and, thus, cost more. 
  2. Specialization. As businesses increasingly rely on data-driven decisions, automation, and advanced technologies, AI/ML developers, data scientists, and DevOps are in high demand and get higher pay. 
  3. Location. Python specialists outside the US and Western Europe offer more cost-effective rates due to lower living costs in those regions. 
  4. Industry. Due to specialized knowledge, work complexity, and expertise in ethical considerations, AI, fintech, healthcare, and game developers cost more.  

Businesses operating in innovative industries need to plan for higher expenses to secure qualified offshore developers.

Offshore Python Development Trends

Let’s look at the main trends driven by technology and business strategies in offshore Python development.

  1. AI and machine learning. According to Statista, the global AI market size is expected to reach $826.73 billion in 2030 compared to $184.04 in 2024, so companies seek offshore Python experts specializing in AI and ML. These specialists help businesses develop and optimize intelligent solutions at reasonable costs while continuously improving their services through new ideas and advanced technologies. 
  2. Adoption of cloud-native technologies. Businesses seek offshore Python talent as demand grows for small, independent services, containerization, and seamless system connections. This helps them use only the computing resources they need.
  3. Integration of DevOps methodologies. DevOps practices speed up software releases, reduce downtime, and allow handling of demand changes. That is why businesses engage DevOps engineers and expect offshore Python devs to implement DevOps methodologies and best practices. 
  4. Expansion of Data Science and Big Data projects. According to the US Bureau of Labor Statistics, the hiring of data scientists is projected to grow by 36% from 2023 to 2033, significantly outpacing the 4% average growth rate for all occupations. Since Python plays a key role in analyzing large datasets, companies hire remote developers for their expertise and cost-effectiveness, using Python to create affordable big data solutions.
  5. Popularity of hybrid offshore and nearshore teams. Organizations tend to build Python development squads in different locations. This lets them save costs with offshore developers while nearshore experts provide better cultural alignment and time zone compatibility. 

These trends show that companies rely more on remote Python developers to cut costs and stay competitive in the fast-evolving, tech-driven business landscape.
